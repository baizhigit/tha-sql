Yes — scan inside the cropper is the right design. Don’t scatter ScannerAgent calls around the app.
Why cropper is the right home for scan
Path	Flow today
File picker	open cropper → crop → UploadPhoto
Scanner	open cropper → scan → crop → UploadPhoto
Webcam	open webcam popup → snap → UploadPhoto (no crop)
PDF	open pdf viewer → upload (no crop)
A raw scanner image almost always needs crop/rotate before submit (same as file upload). Putting “Сканировать” inside cropper reuses that pipeline — no second crop UI, no duplicated upload/callback logic.
Other parts of the app
Your app already has the right pattern: scan buttons elsewhere should open the cropper, not talk to the agent directly.
Evidence in the codebase — UploadDocumentScan() already exists:
